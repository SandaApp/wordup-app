Privacy Policy

Sanda's WordUp — Daily Scripture. Prayer and Growth.
Effective Date: July 1, 2026 · Last Updated: August 13, 2026

Sanda's WordUp is a Bible, prayer, devotional, and spiritual growth app designed to help individuals and families engage with Scripture and prayer.

Information Stored on Your Device

Sanda's WordUp may store the following information locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server, third party, or external service.

  • App settings
  • Reminder preferences
  • Completed devotions
  • Progress and streaks
  • Badge progress
  • Saved verses
  • Highlighted verses
  • Private prayer requests
  • Answered prayer status

This data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to a Sanda's WordUp server in the current version.

Prayer Requests

Prayer requests are private by default. Prayer requests are stored locally on the user's device only. Sanda's WordUp does not share prayer requests with other users, churches, administrators, or third parties.

Bible and Devotional Content

Sanda's WordUp includes Scripture, devotionals, prayers, and spiritual growth content for encouragement and personal or family use. The app currently uses the King James Version Bible text, which is in the public domain.

Notifications

Sanda's WordUp may ask for notification permission so it can send daily Scripture and prayer reminders. You can control notifications through your device settings.

Text-to-Speech

Sanda's WordUp uses the device's built-in text-to-speech system to read Scripture, prayers, and devotionals aloud. This feature runs entirely on your device and does not transmit any audio or text to external servers.

Internet and Data Transmission

Sanda's WordUp is a fully offline application for core features. Opening Privacy Policy, Website, or Support links from Settings will use your device browser and requires internet only for those external pages.

Sanda's WordUp does not use analytics or tracking tools, advertising SDKs, cloud storage or sync, crash reporting services, or third-party login.

Sanda's WordUp does not require account registration. Core functionality is available immediately after installation with no login required.

Permissions

  • Notifications — optional daily Scripture and prayer reminders
  • Exact alarm / schedule — so daily reminders can fire at the time you choose
  • Vibrate — optional notification feedback

Sanda's WordUp does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, photos, or files for core features.

Third-Party Services

Sanda's WordUp does not integrate Google Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, advertising networks, crash reporting platforms, or cloud databases. Sanda's WordUp does not sell user data.

Children and Families

Sanda's WordUp is designed to be family-friendly. Parents and guardians are encouraged to guide children's use of the app.

Data Deletion

Because personal app data is stored locally, users may delete data by clearing the app's storage from Android settings or by uninstalling the app. No personal prayer or progress data exists on an external Sanda's WordUp server to be deleted.

Future Updates

If future versions add accounts, cloud sync, community prayer, or online features, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.
