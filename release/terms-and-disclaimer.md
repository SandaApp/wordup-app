# WORDUP Terms and Disclaimer

Effective Date: To be updated before release

## Purpose

WORDUP is a Bible, prayer, and spiritual growth app designed to help users engage with Scripture and prayer.

## Bible Translation

WORDUP currently uses the King James Version Bible text, which is public domain.

## Spiritual Content Disclaimer

WORDUP provides devotional, prayer, and Bible study content for encouragement and spiritual growth. It is not a replacement for pastoral care, church fellowship, professional counseling, medical advice, or emergency help.

Users facing serious emotional, mental, medical, or safety concerns should seek appropriate professional or pastoral support.

## Prayer Requests

Prayer requests stored in WORDUP are private and local to the device in the MVP version.

## Red-Letter Formatting

WORDUP includes Words of Christ in red as a core feature. Red-letter formatting may be refined over time for accuracy.

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for how they use the app and for reviewing Scripture prayerfully and responsibly.
