# WORDUP Play Store Description

## Short Description

Daily Scripture, prayer, Bible reading, and growth for individuals and families.

## Full Description

WORDUP helps individuals, teens, young adults, adults, and families grow closer to God through daily Scripture, prayer, Bible reading, and spiritual habit-building.

Built around the mission of turning screen time into Scripture time, WORDUP gives users a meaningful alternative to empty scrolling and distraction.

With WORDUP, you can read and search the King James Version Bible offline, listen to Scripture aloud, save and highlight verses, pray guided prayers, track private prayer requests, mark answered prayers, practice memory verses, complete daily devotions, and build consistency through progress tracking and colourful achievement badges.

## Key Features

- Daily devotional readings
- Verse of the day
- Spoken Scripture and prayer
- Offline KJV Bible reader
- Bible search by word, phrase, or reference
- Words of Christ in red
- Saved verses
- Bright verse highlighting
- Guided prayers
- Private prayer requests
- Answered prayer tracking
- Memory verse practice
- Bible quiz questions
- Progress and streak tracking
- Colourful medal-like badges
- Reminder time selection
- Family and individual modes
- Share verses and devotions

WORDUP is designed to be useful for personal devotion, family Bible time, youth encouragement, and everyday spiritual growth.

## Mission Line

Turning screen time into Scripture time.
